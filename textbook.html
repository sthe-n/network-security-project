Description

*Introduction to Network Security* provides a practical and accessible foundation in the principles, technologies, and practices used to protect modern computer networks. The textbook introduces key concepts such as confidentiality, integrity, availability, authentication, access control, encryption, firewalls, intrusion detection, malware, secure network design, and risk management. Designed for students and beginners in Information Technology and Cybersecurity, the book combines theoretical concepts with real-world examples and practical scenarios. Readers will learn how network attacks occur, how vulnerabilities can be identified, and how security controls can be implemented to protect systems, users, and data. By the end of the textbook, learners will have a solid understanding of the network security landscape and the fundamental skills needed to design, evaluate, and maintain more secure networks.
